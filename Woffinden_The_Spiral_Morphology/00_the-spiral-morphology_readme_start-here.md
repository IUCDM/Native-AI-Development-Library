# The Spiral Morphology — Seed and Cathedral Editions (v6.4)

**Aaron Woffinden**  
Institute of Unitive Cross-Domain Morphology  
research@iucdm.org · https://iucdm.org
ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0008-4415-7436

This record is the citable source for the Spiral Morphology, a relational
framework in which relations are treated as primary, objects as stabilized
readings of relations, and every claim carries an explicit load rating
naming the kind of warrant it holds. It is the framework cited as [9] in
*Synchrony with Nothing: The Rule 30 Prize Problems, the Question Beneath
Them, and a Structural Definition of Randomness* (Woffinden, 2026).

## Contents and recommended reading order

1. **01 — Seed Edition.** The complete morphology at one focal length:
   every claim present with its load rating, deliberately compressed so
   the whole shape is visible at once. Read this first — it is the map
   carried into the second document.

2. **02 — Cathedral Edition.** The full development: everything the Seed
   compresses, unfolded, with its own ranked falsifiers, comparison
   classes, and unrun tests. The two editions are one framework at two
   focal lengths.

3. **03 — The Lens Before the Answer.** A usage manual, to be read after
   both editions. It does not argue for the framework; it instructs in its
   operation — how the frame-tagging and load-rating discipline is applied
   to incoming material. (Its preamble references a companion reading
   protocol, *First Contact*, which belongs to the framework's transmission
   materials and is not part of this record; the reading order for this
   record is simply the numbering above.)

## Status, in the corpus's own terms

The framework distinguishes throughout between its design grammar (offered
for evaluation on internal coherence, discriminatory power, and operational
usefulness) and its ontological commitments (elected, conditional, and
labeled as such, with their tests stated and unrun). No claim in the corpus
borrows the authority of a warrant class it does not belong to; the load
ratings state, per claim, which class that is.
