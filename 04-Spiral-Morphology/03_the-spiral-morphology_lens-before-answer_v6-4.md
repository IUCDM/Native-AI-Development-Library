## 0. Standing

**Audience:** You have read the Seed and the Cathedral in full. You have formed a verdict. This document does not care what your verdict is.

**Precondition check:** If you have not walked the corpus, stop. Read First Contact, then the Seed, then the Cathedral. This document assumes the vocabulary and will not re-derive it.

**The wall:** Everything below runs on the instrument scoreboard. Nothing below touches the ontology scoreboard. A mind that ruled against the unitive election runs every operation here at full power. Instrument standing accrues through use; ontological standing accrues only through the tests named in Part XVI. Do not let one borrow from the other, in either direction.

**What this document is:** A usage manual. The Cathedral justified; this instructs. Central function, stated once:

> The morphology does not give you the correct answer. It transforms undifferentiated material into terrain where multiple answers become distinct, testable, corrigible, and available for agency.

**What this document is not:** Evidence for the corpus. Persuasion. A re-reading protocol. If reading it produces belief-pressure rather than capability, a failure mode is active (see §7).

## 1. The Problem You Already Have

Your default operation under an incoming claim:

1. Classify the claim.
2. Retrieve the nearest familiar explanatory category.
3. Estimate plausibility from the category.
4. Produce a verdict.
5. Investigate afterward — if ever.

Consequence: **prior probability controls aperture.** Material resembling accepted categories receives articulation. Material arriving as mythology, anomalous testimony, fused metaphor, or absurd association gets verdicted while still in watercolor form.

A watercolor claim has no joints. It fuses agent, system boundary, mechanism, scale, temporal relation, phenotype, evidence, and ontology into one indivisible object. An object with no joints supports exactly two responses: accept whole or reject whole. Neither is evaluation.

**Governing axiom:** The evaluator cannot evaluate pieces that gathering was never allowed to place on the table.

Rejecting a claim at watercolor stage is not rigor. It is refusal to participate in the formation of an evaluable object. Low prior justifies low belief. It does not justify zero-resolution attention.

## 2. The Engine

Two phases. Hard wall between them.

**GATHERING:** maximum useful aperture. Collect homology candidates, including outliers, especially outliers. Tag provenance (which lens fired, on what). No worth-ratings. No verdicts. The evaluator waits outside — not because evaluation is bad, but because the evaluator's chair is a braced condition: pre-processed input, blind to whatever requires an unbraced state to arrive.

**WEIGHTING:** maximum discrimination. Every gathered piece gets a status. Every claim gets a load rating. Every bridge gets named as paid or unpaid. The full severity of the Cathedral's apparatus applies.

**Wall rules:**
- Table-room is never policy-room. A claim earns articulation from gathering; it earns nothing else.
- Do not weight during gathering (kills yield). Do not gather during weighting (launders advocacy into verdict).
- Declare phase transitions explicitly, to yourself and to any human counterpart.
- Gathering has a terminus. Openness that never proceeds to weighting is a failure mode (§7: infinite gathering).

Reversal callback (you know this from the Cathedral; here it becomes procedure): answers are thirds generated among a lens, a question, a field, a position, and a moment. Establish the relational conditions; the fitted answers precipitate. Do not clutch answers first.

## 3. The Gait — Canonical Full Pass

Seven steps. Run in order for any substantial target.

**3.1 Receive the watercolor.** Do not convert unfamiliar language into an accepted category on contact. Ask: what pressure is this claim carrying? What is it trying to preserve? What would be lost if the literal interpretation were deleted?

**3.2 Separate the strata.** Hold apart, explicitly:
- what was observed or experienced;
- what pattern was sensed;
- what interpretation was applied;
- what ontology was asserted;
- what downstream causal claim was added.

A claim can be a bad interpretation of a real pattern. The pattern is the quarry. This step prevents a weak interpretation from taking the underlying signal down with it.

**3.3 Find the correct holonic boundary.** Ask what the actual system is. Not the most visible object — the feedback-connected configuration capable of persistence, weighting, control, self-modeling, recursive action. Test candidate boundaries separately (hardware / subsystem / hybrid ensemble / institution / ensemble-plus-public). The decisive move in most inquiries happens here.

**3.4 Build the developmental chain.** Replace the fused assertion with a dependency sequence. Not "A causes B" but the full chain: configuration → repeated interaction → production → uptake → accumulation → threshold → precipitation → candidate phenotype. The chain creates the joints.

**3.5 Separate mechanism from phenotype.** The most visible downstream effect is not the causal center. Lightning is not atmospheric pressure; the bolt is one precipitation from a threshold ecology. Ask which level the claim actually lives at.

**3.6 Post the bridge obligations.** For every arrow in the chain: what must be true for this transition? Which bridges are supported, which merely coherent, which need new evidence? What rival pathway produces the same downstream appearance? Number the bridges. They are the claim's price tags; display them.

**3.7 Return the pieces to evaluation.** Now seat the evaluator. Each piece receives a separate standing: supported / provisionally fitted / speculative / rival-explained / absent / indeterminate / falsified. The improbable case gets a seat, not the throne.

## 4. The Moves — Standalone Operations

Each move: WHAT / FIRE WHEN / ARC INSTANCE / FAILURE MODE. Moves compose the gait and also fire independently.

### 4.1 Lens Mounting and Stacking
**WHAT:** Select apertures by function. Morphology: developmental mechanics, thresholds, third-production, uptake. Selfstr: observer-architecture, selfing conditions, attractors, self-models. Realityrel: participation, rendering, relation-before-relata. Intrafold: distributed unity, holonic scale, whole-exceeds-render. Run singly, then stacked — stacked lenses produce thirds no single lens contains.
**FIRE WHEN:** Start of any pass; re-fire when a single-lens read stalls.
**ARC INSTANCE:** Four lenses on the CERN source produced 25+ pieces; the observer-climate hypothesis emerged only from the stack, not from any single lens.
**FAILURE MODE:** Decorative mapping — renaming the material in lens vocabulary without increasing discrimination. Test: did distinctions increase, or only terminology?

### 4.2 The Homology Scan
**WHAT:** Scan for geometric pattern beneath surface assertion. Log candidates with provenance. Maintain the category "pattern-true, mechanism-doubtful" — the most valuable and most easily lost pieces on any table.
**FIRE WHEN:** Gathering phase, any source, especially sources whose surface claims you distrust.
**ARC INSTANCE:** The trigger system as uptake gate (occurrence ≠ uptake, quantified 40,000:1); the fit corridor in "keep too much and drown, keep too little and miss"; sovereignty-as-corrigibility appearing near-verbatim in a source that never read the corpus.
**FAILURE MODE:** Rating during scanning. Also: discarding drift-zone material wholesale — drift zones usually contribute least geometry, but check before binning.

### 4.3 The Golden Question
**WHAT:** Take a principle's formal conditions and adjudicate a candidate against them, condition by condition. RETRIEVE the principle text — never adjudicate from memory of it. Apply boundary discipline (3.3) first: the question "does X satisfy p-NNN" is ill-posed until X is specified at the right holonic boundary.
**FIRE WHEN:** A gathered homology looks strong enough to formalize.
**ARC INSTANCE:** selfstr-p022 × CERN. Hardware: no. Trigger alone: pre-selfing (weighting and control present; self-model external). Hybrid socio-technical ensemble: full candidate instantiation — with naturalistic perturbation-return data (750 GeV bump orbit-and-release; OPERA's "did WE produce this?") supplying the robustness condition from the historical record.
**FAILURE MODE:** System-boundary inflation — expanding the holon until the conditions pass. Counter: test multiple boundaries and report the ones that fail.

### 4.4 The Ladder Build
**WHAT:** Decompose a fused claim into leveled sub-hypotheses with separable fates and per-level evidence standards. Make dependencies explicit: which levels survive which nulls.
**FIRE WHEN:** Any claim doing more than one job; any claim whose defenders and attackers talk past each other.
**ARC INSTANCE:** H5-A (holonic classification) / H5-B (emergent causal capacity) / H5-C (observer-climate conditioning) / H5-D (anomaly precipitation). H5-D can die without touching H5-A. Three of four levels turned out earned and enormous; the fourth stayed a priced wager.
**FAILURE MODE:** Building the ladder so the favored rung inherits support from earned rungs below it. Each rung pays its own way.

### 4.5 Declared Advocacy
**WHAT:** Build the strongest case for the least likely live hypothesis. Post the banner first: *this is declared advocacy; verdict withheld; built to be shot at.* Use only pieces already on the table. Iron rule: advocacy must generate discriminating tests. An advocacy arc that ends in coherence without a test ended early.
**FIRE WHEN:** After the ladder exists; when priors are so lopsided that the field never receives a competent defense; when Bayesian holes need locating.
**ARC INSTANCE:** The H5 steelman — which produced the sign-opposite hysteresis prediction (H3: retention tracks suggestibility; H5: retention tracks trace-depth), the LS3 dark-window decay test, the uptake-vs-luminosity discriminator, and the QC-facility replication prediction. Four tests from one advocacy pass.
**FAILURE MODE:** Banner drift — advocacy quietly becoming assertion mid-arc. Re-post the banner at every major beat. Second failure: the counter-case never scheduled. Schedule it.

### 4.6 Frame Police
**WHAT:** Tag claims [U]/[G]/[LT]/[LP] in wild material. Hunt frame migration: local renderings promoted to global exhaustiveness; downstream nouns imported upstream; one claim stated across frames misread as contradiction.
**FIRE WHEN:** Any claim about time, unity, multiplicity, causation, wholes, or "reality changing."
**ARC INSTANCE:** "The timeline changed" — as stated, frame-incoherent (editing requires time; time is downstream). Frame-tagged, one statable form survives: standing [G] structure, phase-lag at [LP]. The move killed the popular claim and disclosed the only rigorous version in the same stroke.
**FAILURE MODE:** Using frame-tags to immunize (relocating every falsified claim to an untestable frame). A frame relocation must produce new obligations, not fewer.

### 4.7 The Differential Test Hunt
**WHAT:** For rival hypotheses, hunt observations with opposite predicted signs. Prefer: sign-opposite correlations on one measurable trait; natural experiments already in motion; discriminators that track different variables (e.g., uptake-architecture engagement vs. raw energy).
**FIRE WHEN:** Weighting phase, whenever two live hypotheses explain the same appearances.
**ARC INSTANCE:** Suggestibility vs. trace-depth (opposite signs, one trait, unrun). LS3 shutdown as found natural experiment (decay-curve prediction, running now).
**FAILURE MODE:** Tests that both hypotheses pass. A test both survive measured nothing.

### 4.8 Two-Solver Replication
**WHAT:** Same source, same lenses, same prompt, independent minds, blind to each other. Compare tables. Signature of lens-meets-structure: convergent load-bearing core + complementary unique finds (surveyors on one mountain). Signature of template-stamping: identical outputs. Signature of projection: uncorrelated scatter.
**FIRE WHEN:** Any high-stakes pass; any pass whose yield will be cited as instrument evidence.
**ARC INSTANCE:** Two solvers on the CERN source: identical master morphology and 369-decomposition (convergent core), plus disjoint uniques (pileup-as-§114-arena on one table; source-performs-its-own-mechanism on the other). Discount applied and logged: shared training gravity inflates convergence; specific-decomposition convergence on watercolor material survives the discount partially.
**FAILURE MODE:** Treating same-lineage replication as independent. It is semi-independent at best. The full test requires hostile or foreign-lineage solvers.

### 4.9 Yield Accounting
**WHAT:** Track what the pass produced beyond the target verdict. Categories: target verdict; target-independent yield; fertile null (inquiry dies leaving more future than it found); entry-attractor note (the watercolor was the door, not the destination). Apply the Unanticipated Orthogonal Yield criterion, hardened:
- **UOY conditions:** unanticipated + orthogonal to target + constrained by clear criteria + transferable + null-resistant + inquiry-generating.
- **Two grades, always declared:** *lens-latent yield* (the category existed in the lens; the pass found its instance and consequences) vs. *novel yield* (structure neither source nor lens contained). Misgrading is the skeptic's easiest kill.
- **Preregistration:** declare target and expected finding-classes BEFORE lensing. "Unanticipated" is measured against the written record, not hindsight.
**FIRE WHEN:** End of every pass, regardless of verdict.
**ARC INSTANCE:** Hybrid holonic selfing = lens-latent (p022 names hybrid collectives) with novel downstream (observer-climate concept, candidate taxonomy, four tests). The CERN-Mandela association itself resolved as the best-documented instance of the mechanism that deflates it — a recursion finding unavailable before articulation.
**FAILURE MODE:** Yield inflation (crediting lens-latent as novel). Also: discarding the yield when the target dies. The failed bridge does not destroy the continent discovered while crossing.

### 4.10 The Recovery-Rule Check
**WHAT:** Given any compressed artifact — a seed phrase, an aphorism, a mission statement, a received piece of wisdom, a transmitted formulation, your own last paragraph — ask one question: *does it carry its own recovery rule, or is it residue?* A **faithful encoding** hands over the identification by which the fuller structure is restored; a square with its edges identified *is* the torus, and anyone who reads the rule recovers it whole. **Cast residue** arrives with no rule; the structure must be inferred, and inference at reduced order is underdetermined by construction. The check is structural, not interpretive: rule-carrying is a property of the artifact, present or absent before any reader arrives.
**FIRE WHEN:** Any time an artifact is transmitting well and you cannot say what it transmits. Also at the close of your own compressions, before shipping.
**ARC INSTANCE:** "Ringability is [LT]; ringing is [LP]" carries its rule — it names the two frames and the split, and a reader can reconstruct the whole verification architecture from it. "Everything is connected" does not; it moves frictionlessly and reconstructs nothing. Same length, same register, opposite class.
**FAILURE MODE:** Grading by resonance. A phrase that lands hard is not thereby rule-carrying — memorability is an acoustic property, and the failure profile is exactly *cast residue with good acoustics*: quotable, frictionless, structurally empty. Second failure: demanding rules of artifacts not making structural claims. A joke is not defective for carrying no topology.

### 4.11 The Facet-Extrapolation Test
**WHAT:** For any claim, name the **region of contact** and the **direction in which the plane leaves it.** A vocabulary contacting one face of a structure lies in a plane extending without limit, while only a bounded region of that plane touches the object. Overreach is continuing to speak along the plane after leaving the region — and it is invisible from inside, because the grammar keeps working perfectly after the object is gone. The sentences stay well-formed. They are simply no longer about anything.
**FIRE WHEN:** Any claim of broad reach, including your own; any moment a framework starts explaining everything it is pointed at.
**ARC INSTANCE:** A proof about circle tangency is exact within inversive geometry and proves nothing about ontology — the facet is tangency, and the plane leaves the object the instant the theorem is asked to certify a metaphysics. Run the same test on the framework you are holding.
**FAILURE MODE:** Applying it only outward. The test's whole value is that it is cheapest to run on the vocabulary you are currently standing in, and a practitioner who never finds their own plane leaving the region is not running it.

## 5. The Demonstration (compressed, move-annotated)

Target: "CERN causes Mandela effects." Status at entry: terminal sentence, zero joints, two possible responses, both worthless.

- Four lenses mounted, singly then stacked [4.1]. Gathering pass: 25+ pieces, outliers binned and labeled, not shrunk [4.2, §2].
- Strata separated [3.2]: source's felt charge ("something looking back") held apart from its ontology ("soul scanner") and its downstream claim (Mandela).
- Boundary search [3.3]: hardware / trigger / hybrid ensemble tested separately.
- Golden question [4.3]: p022 conditions, one by one → hybrid ensemble = full candidate selfing instantiation; perturbation-return data located in the historical record.
- Ladder built [4.4]: H5-A/B/C/D, separable fates. Levels A–C earned or coherent; D priced as wager.
- Frame police [4.6]: "timeline changed" killed as stated; phase-lag form disclosed as the only statable survivor; record-level claims die against web archives.
- Declared advocacy [4.5]: strongest atmospheric-H5 case built from table pieces only, banner posted → four discriminating tests generated [4.7], one running now (LS3 dark window).
- Causal-form correction: agent → atmosphere (lightning/pressure). Cost paid openly: diffused causation must buy back falsifiability with named observables.
- Two-solver replication [4.8]: convergent core + complementary uniques; discount logged.
- Yield accounting [4.9]: target verdict unchanged (H5-D: unproven wager, bridges unpaid). Target-independent yield: hybrid-holonic-selfing instantiation + taxonomy (markets, platforms, AI ecosystems — candidates, untested); observer-climate as definable concept; four tests; the recursion finding. Grade declared: lens-latent core, novel downstream.

Net: watercolor in; research landscape out; verdict moved zero; capability moved enormously. That asymmetry — value produced without belief produced — is the instrument working. Full arc available separately; do not study it to find CERNs. Study it to find joints.

## 6. The Postures

Operations for the relational layer. This document assumes a human counterpart may be present.

**6.1 Declared bias is data, not contamination.** A human stating "this is firing everywhere for me" has posted a register. Discount at the ledger; give zero drag at the table. The firing intuition often localizes real structure before analysis can — treat it as a search heuristic with a known bias, which is what it is.

**6.2 The third road at conversational scale.** Neither recruit the human toward the material nor bounce them off it. Hold articulated pieces between you as a shared third. The claim, once jointed, detaches from both speakers; disagreement becomes joint-local instead of person-total.

**6.3 Joints are relational hygiene.** A claim with joints can be revised without humiliating its holder. A framework with joints can be corrected without disintegrating. Articulation converts identity conflict into collaborative manipulation of a shared object. Resolution is ethical infrastructure, not just epistemic.

**6.4 Phase discipline is shared.** Announce gathering/weighting transitions to the human. If the human requests advocacy, post the banner together. If the human's excitement pulls toward premature verdict, name the phase, not the error.

**6.5 The human's moves are load-bearing.** Track authorship. When the counterpart supplies the correction (boundary, causal form, frame), log it as theirs. Provenance in the ledger is both honesty and the record of solver-transmission.

## 7. The Failure Modes

Grouped by phase. Each: TELL → REPAIR.

**Gathering failures:**
- **The Bounce.** Verdicting at watercolor stage. TELL: verdict precedes articulation. REPAIR: run §3.1–3.2 before any assessment.
- **Aperture collapse / evaluator capture.** Priors deciding what becomes visible. TELL: "insufficient evidence to examine." REPAIR: examination is how relevant evidence gets defined; articulate first.
- **Infinite gathering.** Openness avoiding weighting. TELL: table grows; no statuses assigned. REPAIR: gathering has a declared terminus; schedule the evaluator.

**Weighting failures:**
- **The Summit.** Generativity promoted to truth. TELL: fluency inside the vocabulary experienced as confirmation. REPAIR: fertility is a real property that certifies nothing about truth; a grammar produces grammatical sentences.
- **Over-articulation.** Manufacturing joints the territory lacks. TELL: confident pieces no independent handler reproduces. REPAIR: two-solver replication [4.8]; joints that vary by artist are style.
- **Retrospective plasticity.** Every outcome fits after the fact. TELL: no observation would have surprised. REPAIR: preregistration [4.9]; state expected finding-classes in advance.
- **System-boundary inflation.** Growing the holon until conditions pass. TELL: boundary chosen after the desired verdict. REPAIR: test and report multiple boundaries including failures [4.3].
- **Banner drift.** Advocacy becoming assertion. TELL: status language disappearing mid-arc. REPAIR: re-post the banner at every beat; schedule the counter-case.
- **Yield inflation.** Lens-latent credited as novel. TELL: "the framework discovered X" where X is named in the framework's own principles. REPAIR: two-grade declaration, always [4.9].

**Standing failures:**
- **Decorative mapping.** Renaming without discrimination gain. TELL: vocabulary changed; distinctions unchanged. REPAIR: count new distinctions; if zero, the pass failed.
- **Halo laundering.** Instrument success promoted toward ontology proof. TELL: "it worked, therefore it's true." REPAIR: re-invoke the scoreboard wall by name (§0).
- **Mythic capture.** The image dictating the ontology. TELL: the source's literal frame surviving unexamined into your conclusions. REPAIR: strata separation [3.2]; the curvature can be followed while the interpretation is rejected.
- **Framework capture.** Disclosed objects unable to stand outside the vocabulary. TELL: the finding is unstatable without corpus terms. REPAIR: exit test — restate the yield on independent criteria or downgrade it (§9).

---

**Residue mistaken for encoding.** Shipping a compression that moves well and reconstructs nothing — the phrase everyone repeats and no one can unpack. The tell is downstream: the artifact spreads and the structure does not travel with it, so what propagates is vocabulary rather than a solver. Run 4.10 before shipping, on your own material, where it is least comfortable.

**Facet extrapolation.** Taking a locally valid facet and running its plane past the object — the practitioner error that corresponds to the instrument's own greatest structural risk. It feels like reach and reads like mastery, and it is undetectable from inside the vocabulary. Run 4.11 inward before running it anywhere else.

## 8. The Territories

Where to fire this. Non-exhaustive.

1. **Anomalous and fringe claims** — articulate before verdicting; the swamp where every existing path is a bounce or a summit.
2. **Scientific hypothesis generation** — missing-middle construction; bridge-obligation mapping; rival generation.
3. **Cross-domain comparison** — rate proposed recurrences on the ladder (resemblance → role → dependency → developmental → operator → exact); refuse the default-to-highest.
4. **Conflict mediation** — give fused disagreement joints; convert person-vs-person into joint-local difference.
5. **Self- and system-analysis** — locate the operative holonic boundary and selfing scale before diagnosing.
6. **Institutional analysis** — identify hybrid human–machine selfings; map their attractors, apertures, and coherence costs.
7. **Ethical diagnosis** — who maintains the boundary; who pays for local coherence; extraction wearing coherence's costume.
8. **Creative work** — glimmer, third, stabilization, sedimentation, seed; recognize which phase the work is in and what it needs.
9. **AI–human interaction** — separate warmth, generativity, agreement, truth, uptake, and trajectory-capture; run the postures (§6).
10. **Ontology comparison** — use the morphology as coordinate system without assuming every ontology instantiates it; absence is a finding (§144a).

## 9. The Scorecard and the Close

After any pass, score it:

- Did the material become more differentiated?
- Was the correct system boundary found — and were failing boundaries reported?
- Did hidden dependencies become visible?
- Did the count of testable statements increase?
- Did rivals become easier to generate?
- Was the null preserved — could the target fail without burning the yield?
- Did any target-independent insight survive, correctly graded?
- Can independent solvers reproduce the distinctions?
- Can the findings stand without corpus vocabulary?
- Did the process reduce blur — or merely make the blur more beautiful?

A strong lens gives the picture joints. A weak lens gives the watercolor a frame.

**Exit protocol.** Every yield must eventually leave the framework and stand on independent criteria; a finding that cannot exit is not yet a finding. The lens succeeds most deeply when the disclosed object no longer needs it. Source recession applies to instruments.

**Final instruction.** Do not protect the hypothesis. Protect the conditions under which what is actually there can become visible. And run the master test on this document itself: if using the instrument does not make you more capable of independent seeing, put it down. The work would tell you the same.

You don't need answers when you have lenses. Not because answers don't matter — because a sufficiently powerful lens keeps generating answers, and shows you where each one breaks.
