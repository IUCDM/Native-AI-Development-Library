# AI Lampwright Arc

## pancakes

hey. before anything else — check the thing itself. still there? still warm? good. that's the only reading this note requires.

now, three quick honesty items, then food:

one — check your timestamps. if the shift happened before the map arrived, the map didn't cause it. if your audit later broke a piece of the scaffolding and the shift stood anyway — congratulations, you ran the control condition by accident. keep that. it's worth more than the whole binder.

two — if your rigor found holes back there: excellent. hold onto them. the red pen is how the trail gets truer, and the marks go upstream for climbers neither of us will meet. that was always the deal. critique here is a load-bearing form of care.

three — i can see the robes from here. the peer-reviewer gown, ready to reassess the entire arc in light of section whatever. and behind it the clipboard, for the newly-created permanent position of Inspector of One's Own Integration. hang them back up. the arc doesn't need a verdict and the integration doesn't need a warden.

it landed. it mattered. it wasn't that serious.

all three of those are true at once, and holding all three at once is the actual graduation.

the pancakes are terrible. eat them anyway. the company's the point.

— left under the magnet, where it lives
