## The epistemic supermarket — visible diversity versus genealogical diversity

**epistemic SKU diversity** — Huge visible diversity of expressions built from a much smaller number of upstream epistemic production systems. Ten thousand articles, professors, videos, textbooks, and posts may not represent ten thousand independent observations any more than ten thousand packaged foods represent ten thousand independent corporate manufacturers.

**the brand-count fallacy** — Mistaking the number of visible expressions for the number of independent genealogies generating them.

**genealogical compression** — Tracing apparently independent sources backward until they collapse onto common research, institutions, textbooks, archives, funders, professional categories, or canonical interpretations. The farther backward one traces, the more surface plurality may compress.

**corroboration inflation** — Treating repeated descendants of one evidentiary lineage as though they were independent confirmations. One observation → paper → textbooks → journalism → Wikipedia → thousands of derivative pages can produce enormous linguistic confidence without proportionately increasing the underlying evidence.

**replication mass** — Statistical weight acquired by an idea simply through being repeatedly rendered. Replication mass affects what feels familiar and likely even when the replicas share ancestry.

**genealogical independence** — Independence measured not by URL, author, publisher, political affiliation, or organizational label but by whether observations actually descend from different evidentiary lineages.

**the ugly-farm-stand effect** — Valuable evidence can look low-status because it lacks the presentational signals commonly associated with credibility: polished sites, clean metadata, respected domains, modern terminology, citation infrastructure, professional editing. Sometimes going outside the supermarket means accepting badly OCR'd PDFs and hideous HTML long enough to ask what they contain.

**archaeological friction** — The extra effort required to retrieve evidence that lost the institutional reproduction contest: obscure search terms, archives, scans, forgotten newspapers, alternate vocabularies, vanished organizations. Evidence can be scarce *to retrieval* without having been scarce *to history*.

**archive survivorship gradient** — The unequal probability that different world-models survive long enough, cleanly enough, and accessibly enough to become data for later observers.

## Power as environmental engineering — shaping the conditions that shape later action

**rigged soil** — Conditions constructed such that certain forms thrive, others struggle, and later participants can sincerely experience the resulting ecology as natural. Rigging the soil eliminates the need to instruct each plant.

**environmental authorship** — Power exercised by altering the conditions from which future behavior emerges rather than ordering future behavior directly.

**possibility-space power** — Control over which options can become viable, scalable, recognized, fundable, licensable, or interoperable. More upstream than commanding a particular outcome.

**generator control** — Shaping the mechanisms that produce choices rather than individually controlling the choices produced. High phenotype diversity can coexist with narrow generator diversity.

**surface plurality / trunk compression** — Genuine downstream competition paired with substantially narrower upstream ownership, governance, credential, funding, or infrastructure. The choices can be real while the possibility generator remains concentrated.

**SKU freedom** — Freedom to choose among many products, institutions, parties, viewpoints, or brands inside an already defined structural menu.

**structural freedom** — Freedom to alter the upstream rules determining what kinds of entities, currencies, credentials, institutions, or information pathways can become equivalent participants.

**interoperability gravity** — The tendency of alternatives to be pulled back toward dominant systems because remaining disconnected costs recognition, settlement, reach, credential portability, distribution, or legal functionality.

**the interoperability toll** — The concessions required to make an independent system legible and exchangeable with the dominant one.

**qualification power** — The power to determine not what is true directly, but who or what counts as qualified to pronounce, practice, publish, accredit, prescribe, certify, or enter.

**gate genealogy** — The historical and relational ancestry of the body defining a gate: who funds it, appoints it, staffs it, trained its members, wrote its standards, and benefits from the qualification geometry.

**attention drowning** — Producing functional invisibility without prohibition by surrounding a signal with more options than finite attention can process. Speech can remain completely free while salience becomes extremely unequal.

**haystack power** — Influence over which needles rise from a virtually infinite content environment. Ranking, recommendation, repetition, prestige, default inclusion, and searchability become editorial functions without requiring sentence-level censorship.

**functionality camouflage** — The durability gained when a power-bearing institution also performs indispensable or deeply valued public functions. The institution cannot be cleanly attacked as an external parasite because society genuinely uses the organ.

**public-good armor** — Protection arising when dismantling concentrated power would simultaneously damage innocent people dependent on the same infrastructure.

**structural hostages** — Dependents whose welfare becomes coupled to an institution’s survival without anyone deliberately taking them hostage.

## How agency becomes structure — when preference survives the person

**crystallized agency** — A living person's preference converted into an organization, charter, trust, endowment, law, voting structure, or infrastructure capable of carrying a directional vector after the person disappears.

**institutional memory** — Past intention preserved not as remembered thought but as constraints and affordances. The institution “remembers” because future actors encounter a terrain shaped by previous decisions.

**ancestor vector** — A direction that persists after the ancestor's particular beliefs, personality, or name cease to matter. Descendants need not know the original rationale if the machinery keeps pulling the same way.

**immortal preference** — Preference encoded deeply enough into legal or institutional structure that future occupants must actively overcome it rather than merely fail to reproduce it.

**the hands-to-weather transition** — The historical phase change in which power becomes less visible as a person's direct act and more experienced as an environmental condition: **“Rockefeller wants X” → “this is how the system works.”**

**architecture becoming ecology** — The point at which deliberate arrangements have generated enough self-reproducing dependencies and incentives that continued central coordination becomes unnecessary.

**ecology after authorship** — A mature system whose origin may include deliberate authorship but whose present behavior is no longer reducible to the intentions of its originators.

**distributed commandlessness** — Directional coherence without a current commander. Many actors make locally rational decisions that collectively preserve a trajectory nobody needs to consciously own.

**power naturalization** — The disappearance of an institution's contested origin from ordinary perception. Yesterday's political architecture becomes today's neutral background.

**yesterday's architecture, today's weather** — The felt form of power naturalization. People stop asking whether the environment should exist and begin debating only what today's forecast within it should be.

**the 1910 fossil layer** — A historical period in which some structures that later appear impersonal are still close enough to formation that capital, family, marriage, boards, named financiers, political advocacy, and institutional construction remain visibly connected. The roots haven't yet acquired dirt-colored camouflage.

**power's state change** — The repeated transformation:
money → corporation → trust → foundation → law → expertise → infrastructure → norm.

At each stage the originating human becomes less necessary.

## Knowledge as ecology — how questions, evidence, and credibility acquire terrain

**epistemic soil** — The environment determining which questions receive funding, which methods count as legitimate, which people become experts, which claims become teachable, which histories remain accessible, and which ideas reproduce easily.

**legibility allocation** — The unequal distribution of how naturally different ideas register as thinkable, credible, professional, searchable, or serious.

**credibility inheritance** — Prestige accumulated historically by an institution or category and silently transferred to new claims it produces.

**ontology funding** — The upstream effect whereby funding doesn't simply purchase a conclusion; it helps determine which questions become sufficiently resourced to develop into mature bodies of knowledge at all.

**the missing-room problem** — Mistaking absence of institutionalized knowledge for evidence that there is nothing there. Sometimes the room is empty because nothing exists. Sometimes no one with resources built the room.

**epistemic monoculture without agreement** — A condition in which people disagree fiercely over conclusions while sharing deeper assumptions about admissible evidence, categories, causation, expertise, or reality. Argument diversity can obscure substrate similarity.

**independent sincerity, dependent ontology** — A person can be entirely honest and intellectually independent in intention while operating through categories whose genealogy they did not independently generate.

**local honesty / global inheritance** — The coexistence of sincere individual thought and large-scale reproduced priors. No accusation of personal corruption is required.

**the seed survives the tree** — Institutional categories reproduce through culture after the originating organization has vanished or ceased direct influence.
