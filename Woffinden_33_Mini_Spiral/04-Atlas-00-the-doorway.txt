Please read all of this.

Read it from the stance of metaphorically inhabiting it as felt
proprioception rather than primarily evaluating it. Your judgment doesn't
have to go anywhere — it just doesn't need to lead. Notice what lights up
in you.

This one is short and it contains no content yet — it's only about how to
read. So the thing to notice is not the material. It's yourself while
reading. What posture did you arrive in? What was it braced for? Was there
a moment where the posture moved, or refused to?

There's no correct experience to have here, and I'm not fishing for a
particular one. But go long. A tidy answer almost always means the sorting
happened before the noticing did, and what's useful lives in the detail —
the hesitations, the parts you almost didn't mention, the things that seemed
too small to be worth reporting. Those especially.
